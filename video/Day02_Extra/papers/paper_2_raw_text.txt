1. Introduction
Enterprises will face many risks in the process of development, and financial risk is an inevitable type of risk. 
Once any enterprise has a financial crisis, it will inevitably have a certain impact on the interests of the company, 
investors and the stability of the capital market. In the face of increasingly fierce market competition, on the one 
hand, enterprises in this environment will have a broader space for development, on the other hand, there will also 
be a lot of crises, once the financial crisis occurs, it is likely to lead to bankruptcy of enterprises. Most of the 
companies will face a gradual deterioration of the financial crisis. Therefore, if we can timely find the problems in 
the process of enterprise operation, timely predict the financial crisis signals and possible financial losses of 
enterprises, and ensure that enterprises can timely make effective adjustments to the actual situation in the

Abstract
In order to better solve the problem that the traditional prediction model can not effectively describe the causes of enterprise 
financial crisis, this article uses deep learning model to further improve the effectiveness of enterprise financial distress
prediction. To this end, this article selects A-share (non-financial industry) listed companies in Shanghai and Shenzhen from 
2001 to 2018 as ST sample companies, the financial distress prediction model of listed companies including 23 financial 
indicators based on deep learning was constructed, and the BP algorithm was used to test and verify the model. The prediction
accuracy of the model is 78.33%, and the accuracy of predicting ST company is 82%, which verifies the feasibility of the model 
in predicting financial distress.

2. Literature review
For some analysts, recession is a model for determining whether a company is in financial trouble, and self-
determination is a model for predicting stress finance first [2]. Based on the single financial ratio data of 19 sample 
companies, the financial status of the company is predicted. The warning value of financial indicators is set to 
determine whether the enterprise has fallen into financial distress. At the same time, it was determined that revenues 
(membership equity and membership equity) had the greatest impact on the results observed [3]. Based on data from 
the financial assessments of 79 companies in and out of financial crisis, an estimate of 30 financial indicators was 
assessed using a different determination. The results showed that the estimation of the value of the income / total 
expenses index was the best and the model had accurate accuracy. However, univariate analysis method can only 
use one financial ratio index to predict the financial situation, so it is difficult to determine which index can 
effectively distinguish the financial normal company from the financial distress company, and the model is set too 
simple, so its forecasting ability is very limited[4]. Based on the non-matching principle, the distribution of sample 
companies on the probability interval of bankruptcy, the relationship between the two discriminant errors and the 
segmentation points are studied by using the Logistic nonlinear regression model. The results show that there are no 
less than four kinds of indicators which can greatly affect the probability of bankruptcy of enterprises, and the 
prediction accuracy of bankruptcy can be more than 90% by using these four variables. Through F test on the 
financial index data of bankrupt companies and non-bankrupt companies, the results show that financial leverage, 
rate of return, stock return and fixed income security can significantly affect the accuracy of the model prediction. 
However, this method still has the constraint that collinearity cannot exist between independent variables[5]. Based 
on 70 financially distressed enterprises and 70 financially normal enterprises as research samples, Domestic 
researchers have successfully used Fisher's linear decision analysis, various horizontal modeling, and logistic 
regression analysis to estimate a company's financial position. By comparing the prediction results of the three 
methods, the Logistic regression model has the best prediction effect. It is pointed out that the financial forecast 
using cash flow index is short-term, and the linear probability model and Logistic model are used to predict the 
financial situation respectively. The conclusion is that the earning cash ratio is more important in both models, and 
the Logistic model has better predictive ability[6].

3.2. Selection of research objects
This line will select both good and bad companies with bad financial institutions and NON-ST companies. When 
selecting a timeline survey, this article will select financial statements (t-3) of all company models for t year to 
estimate whether the company will be ST [8]. The reason why according to the process involved in the publication 
of the annual publications of listed companies in the Company of China, the deadline for the publication of the 
publications The annual period of listed companies is within 4 months after the end of the annual financial year. 
Therefore, the time to identify the company in t-year ST and (t-1) annual reports is almost the same. Therefore, it is 
not advisable to use the information in the annual report of the company (t-1) to estimate whether the company will 
be estimated by ST year t. This undoubtedly exaggerates the predictive power of the model and has no effect on 
prior prediction. Therefore, the financial data of all sample companies (t-3) will be selected in this article to predict 
whether the company will be ST in t year, so as to ensure that the trained model can be objective and robust in 
achieving high prediction accuracy [9]. When selecting ST and NON-ST sample companies, the following criteria 
are mainly followed:
In t year, whether ST company or NON-ST company is selected, the financial data of (t-3) year is needed to 
predict the situation of T year. Therefore, it is necessary to ensure that all companies in the sample of t years have 
been listed at least in (t-3) years, otherwise it is impossible or difficult to obtain financial indicator data.
(1) Due to the particularity of financial companies, financial companies are excluded from the sample companies 
in this study;
(2) When selecting the sample of ST companies, only the companies that are ST for the first time are selected, 
that is, the companies change from normal to ST state for the first time since listing. For example, a company was 
ST for the first time in 2001, and then became normal and experienced ST for many times, then only the financial 
data of the company in 1998 were selected to predict the state of the company in 2001.
(3) When selecting the sample of ST company, other companies that are not in abnormal financial condition are

4. A study of financial stress estimates of listed companies based on in-depth research standards
4.1. Sample Selection
In this article, A total of 465 listed companies in Shanghai and Shenzhen A-shares (non-financial industries) from 
2001 to 2018 were selected as ST sample companies because of their abnormal financial conditions and were 
specially treated for the first time. A total of 2325 companies that have never been ST from 2001 to 2018 were 
selected as NON-ST sample companies with 5 times the number of ST companies each year. 
In addition, when NON-ST sample companies are selected every year, the overall number of selected companies 
is 5 times that of ST sample companies. This article also makes statistics on the number of companies in various 
industries (non-financial industries) that are first ST listed every year, and then selects samples of NON-ST 
companies in corresponding industries with the number of ST companies in each industry 5 times.

5. Conclusion 
Compared with the traditional financial distress prediction model, the deep learning model can depict the real 
world from linear to nonlinear and from shallow learning to deep learning. This improvement enables the deep 
learning model to depict the nonlinear complex relationship between high-dimensional data and the hidden features, 
and finally achieve the purpose of feature judgment. Based on this, this article constructs a five-layer 
(23,18,14,10,5,2) deep learning network model, using the 23-dimensional financial index data of Shanghai and 
Shenzhen A-share (non-financial) listed companies (T-3) in t years to predict their financial status (whether ST). 
From the results, the deep learning model is obviously superior to the Logistic model and BP neural network model 
in both the ability of predicting ST companies and NON-ST companies and the overall forecasting ability of the 
model. Especially in the prediction of ST Company, the prediction accuracy of the deep learning model is as high as 
82%. Which indicates that the deep learning model constructed and trained in this article can be fully applied to the 
financial distress prediction of listed companies. Moreover, compared with the traditional Logistic model and BP 
neural network model, it has a stronger ability of three years' early warning.
