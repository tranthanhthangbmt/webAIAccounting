2.9 Security and Tokens
DLT and the blockchain empower the digitization of any asset class as
tokens and are shaping the manner toward future of financial markets.
Security token offerings (STOs) are advancing funding, where digital
tokens are issued as regulated investment products on the blockchain. Once
the token offering has taken place, the tokens are traded on the secondary
market. Does the uncontrolled offering of digital money without any
examination of its value generate over-offering of money leading to
devaluation?
Security tokens118 denote shares of ownership in corporate equity, fixed
income, investment funds, commodities, or less liquid asset classes such as
real estate or fine art, comprising a new form of venture financing having

several advantages because corporations easily reach a large investor base
while dropping transaction costs.
First of all, transparency concerning the transactions is accomplished
through the blockchain, and the interoperability of the blockchain solves the
problem of lack of compatibility between different systems or databases,
allowing self-custody of any tokenized asset on one platform.119 Another
major advantage of STOs is the prospective liquidity offered through the
likelihood to transfer and trade tokens on secondary markets, which means
that security tokens merge the advantages of the underlying technology with
the legal protection of conventional securities.
Security token offerings are a method for corporations to increase capital
where they issue digital tokens as regulated investment products on the
blockchain. By transferring a digital token, the participants transfer the
ownership or other property rights over the asset represented by the token
without the intervention of traditional intermediaries. J. Kreppmeier and R.
Laschinger120 argue that
“companies that intend to raise funding via STOs would be
well advised to offer a pre-sale phase in their STO and assure
their intentions to trade the tokens on the secondary market
while already devising a plan for successful future trading.
From an investor’s perspective, these signals can be
interpreted as positive quality signals on the basis of which
appropriate investment decisions are conducted”.
STOs allow investment in securities recorded on a blockchain. In line,
Thomas Lambert et al.121 argue that “entrepreneurs should carefully design
their security tokens and run their offering professionally because doing so
may not just help them raise more capital, but it may also foster general
awareness of the benefits of a well-developed STO market.”
2.10 Crypto Assets

A crypto asset is a digital asset that functions through the digitalized
distributed ledger or the blockchain. Crypto assets have appeared as a key
movement in digital transformation and, by utilizing new technologies, are
distinguished as one method of advancing financial operations. Moreover,
crypto assets are being integrated into financial services applications to
make possible near real-time transactions, accurate data recording, and
more efficient payment processes. Hence, crypto assets create efficiency
advantages in the financial sector by transforming the performance of
financial markets to enable the exchange of value without the necessity for
a central authority or intermediary, which means that crypto assets generate
openings for the financial sector.
It could be said that crypto-assets markets are fast advancing and
reaching a point where they will epitomize a threat to global financial
stability as a result of their scale, structural vulnerabilities, and augmenting
interconnectedness with the conventional financial system. Also, the swift
evolution and global quality of these markets create the prospect for
regulatory gaps, fragmentation, or arbitrage.
The emergence of Bitcoin and other cryptocurrencies has generated an
anonymous alternative financial system, which means that criminal
networks now have new openings to circumvent detection and seizure of
funds. Nonetheless, crypto is hardly ever used as a medium of exchange to
purchase goods and services and so is normally converted from and back to
fiat currencies, such as US dollars, which means that the entry and exit
points into the crypto ecosystem are crypto exchanges adhering to
international laws designed to mitigate illicit financial flows. Also, while
cryptocurrencies are aimed to be anonymous, the environment in which the
transactions clear on the blockchain presents a ledger that tracks the
movement of funds; thus, blockchain transactions are quasi-anonymous
which means that, via algorithms, it is clarified where funds enter, move
through, are exchanged into different cryptocurrencies, and exit the crypto
ecosystem.122 Thus, cryptocurrencies are another cost-effective network for
moving illicit funds across borders. To that extent, John M. Griffin and
Kevin Mei123 indicate that “the network of scammer nodes is highly
connected, likely indicating that there exist widely-used services that funnel
funds for extremely large and possibly related criminal networks.” It seems

that clustering AAI algorithms can be used to detect Bitcoin transactions for
moving funds across sovereignties.
