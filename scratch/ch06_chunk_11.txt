PAC 6.2  Auditing: Analyze Potential Fraud
Data   Auditing   D*Tunes’ policy is that potential customers can have a free initiation lesson. How-
ever, instructors are still paid for these lessons. The owner does not want to change this policy, but under-
stands that it creates an opportunity for fraud. You are asked to analyze the following four questions.
Build an information model to explore each question. Then, create a report for each of them.
	 1.	 What is the cost of the initiation lessons. What investment has been made thus far?
	 2.	 Are there students that took more than one initiation lesson?
	 3.	 Are there instructors with an unusually high number of initiation lessons?
	 4.	 What is the conversion rate? That is, how many students who signed up for an initiation lesson also 
eventually paid for a lesson or a Friday night party?
