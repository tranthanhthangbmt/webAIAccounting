STEP 5: To address data issues resulting from nicknames, typos, and reversed names, click the 
checkbox Use fuzzy matching to perform the merge. Fuzzy matching is an algorithm that 
measures similarities between two sets of values. The similarity threshold determines 
how similar the values need to be for them to match. To fully match both sets here, the 
threshold should be lowered to 0.4. Click OK to perform the merge.
