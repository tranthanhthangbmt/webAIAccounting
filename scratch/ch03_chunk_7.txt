The primary professional accounting practice areas have both com-

strategic, operational, and compliance motivations.

d.	 needing to perform evaluation on processes or performance.

a.	 stakeholders, purpose, alternatives, risks, knowledge, and

d.	 stakeholders, acquiring knowledge, alternatives, and self- 
reflection.
e.	 stakeholders, risk management, alternatives, and self- 
reflection.
5.  (LO 1)  Analyses of the stakeholders and their perspectives helps to 
critically think through motivation to perform data analyses because
a.	 it reminds you who pays your salary.

c.	 considering the perspectives of stakeholders helps identify 
the objective and develop good questions.
d.	 internal stakeholders should never be considered.

critical thinking.

a.	 keeps us focused on the reason why something is done or created.

a.	 using bar charts from last year’s operational results.
b.	 starting with the results you want to achieve.
c.	 paying others to help you.
d.	 acquiring and applying new knowledge.
e.	 None of these help you perform better data analyses.
8.  (LO 1)  Critically thinking through the risks to your perspective as

minimize
a.	 errors from biases.
b.	 errors from lack of experience.
c.	 errors from your assumptions.
d.	 faulty conclusions from errors or omissions in your data.
