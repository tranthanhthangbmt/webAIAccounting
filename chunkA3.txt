1.7 AI, Decentralized Finance (DeFi), and NFTs
AI’s capability to produce distinctive content through algorithm s displays a
novel approach  to NFT formation, where digital artworks are created
autonomously or in collaborati on with human artists. AI is not only an
instrument for producing NFTs but also for managing them, which means
that advanced algorithms investigate market trends, user preferences, and
other data to propose NFTs to prospective buyers, thus augmenting the
discovery process in marketplaces. Hence, AI competencies are seen as an
instrumental asset for both creators seeking visibility and collectors looking
for their next acquisition. In other words, AI’s function in NFTs allows
confirming the authenticity and originality of digital assets, which means
that AI aids in identifying prosp ective copyright infringements or duplicates
and so warrant ing the integrity of the marketplace. Nonetheless, the
employment of AI in the NFT space causes concerns regarding  authorship
and copyright, particularly when AI-generated artworks becom e tokenized
as NFTs. Thus, while AI technology progresses, its impulsion on the NFT
ecosystem is increasing and restructuring the terrain of digital creativity and
ownership.93

It has to be taken into accou nt that the synthesis of Decentralized
Finance (DeFi) and NFTs94 denotes  a considerable evolution in the digital
economy , and so, this conver gence presents innovative financial products
and services, escalating the utility of NFTs in finance. DeFi platforms
employ blockchain technology to generate financial systems that run
without central authorities, such as banks or governments, presenting
services like lending, borrowing , and investing directly on the blockchain.
To that extent, NFTs are becoming fundamental to DeFi by representing
distinctive asset s utilized as collateral for loans or as part of investment
strategies. In other words, an NFT art piece employed as collateral to secure
a loan on a DeFi platform opens up new liquidity prospects for NFT
holders, permitt ing them to leverage their digital assets without selling
them. Hence, DeFi projects tokenize real-world assets as NFTs, making
them tradable and functional within the DeFi ecosystem, and so, tokenizing
property titles, luxury goods, or unique experiences, which means that
NFTs expand involvement in DeFi, advancing more diverse investment
opportunities. Nonetheless, amalgamating NFTs with DeFi brings forward
complexities, such as evaluating the value of irreplaceable digital assets for
collateral reason s and handling the risks linked with their volat ility. Hence,
the decentralized character of these platforms presents extra challenges in
safeguarding security and averting fraud, and so, demanding constant
innovation in design and platform governance. In line, Chen Jun and Danny
Friedmann95 argue that
“NFT  platforms explicitly point out that if one buys a “digital
collectible,” one will get the NFT  in combination with
copyright of the underlying work. Its monetization process is
linked to fiat money (namely the Renminbi), but the
tradability of NFT s is limited to trade within separate
platforms/chains.”
