[74]. Continuous testing and validation are necessary to identify weak­

systems that can survive malicious input while also continuously improv­
ing fraud detection algorithms to fend off new dangers. The cornerstone of 
protecting the financial sector from fraud is model robustness, which also 
supports the dependability and credibility of AI and ML systems [75].
