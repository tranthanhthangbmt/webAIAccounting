1.15 Blockchain and AI in Finance

technology and AI is steering in a new age of security and transparency

transparency . Blockchain has to make certain that once data is recorded, it

security that seems to be challenged by new technologies. Therefore,
blockchain must become an ideal choice for reinforcing financial systems
against cyber threats and fraudulent activities. On the other hand, AI
contributes predictive analytics and automation to the forefront  of financial

making, heighte ning processes and uplifting overall effective ness. Thus,
